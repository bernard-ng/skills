## What it does

Turns verified literature into a bounded, answerable gap, problem, or prediction and connects it to the aim.

It states the gap as a statement, not a question, and refuses global absence claims without a search.

## When to reach for it

- **Invocation.** Model-invoked: the agent reaches for it when the task fits, or you can name it.
- **Trigger boundary.** Reach for this when a paper needs its reason to exist. It follows [literature-review-positioning](./literature-review-positioning.md).

## Common questions

**How do I criticise earlier work politely?**
Describe limits of the method or scope, in impersonal, hedged wording.

## It's working if

- The gap follows from cited evidence and matches the study's scope.
- The Discussion can say how far it was answered.

## Where it fits

Between literature and aim in [write-introduction](./write-introduction.md). Map: [router](./science-research-writing.md).
