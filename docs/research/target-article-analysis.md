## What it does

Learns a journal's conventions from three or four sample articles and the author guide, by labelling what each sentence does rather than what it says, then building a move model and a phrase bank.

It records conventions as evidence and never copies sample text.

## When to reach for it

- **Invocation.** Model-invoked: the agent reaches for it when the task fits, or you can name it.
- **Trigger boundary.** Reach for this when journal or field conventions are unknown, or when asked to "write like the journals in my field". It fires automatically before drafting if no samples were consulted.

## Common questions

**What if I have no sample articles?**
It states the defaults it assumed and asks for samples. It will not invent articles.

**Why label function, not content?**
A label such as "states a gap" transfers to your own paper; "says the polymer is weak" does not.

## It's working if

- You get a convention sheet: headings, move model per section, tense and voice norms, phrase bank by function.
- Differences between journals are recorded, not blended.
