## What it does

Writes a stand-alone abstract from the finished paper, in a structured or compact shape, with specific results and the exact terms readers would search for.

It adds nothing the paper does not contain.

## When to reach for it

- **Invocation.** Model-invoked: the agent reaches for it when the task fits, or you can name it.
- **Trigger boundary.** Reach for this once the paper is stable, or to cut an abstract to a limit. Titles are separate: [write-title](./write-title.md).

## Common questions

**Which shape do I use?**
The journal usually decides. Narrow fields lean compact, broader ones structured.

**May I cite in an abstract?**
Rarely. Only when the paper directly extends or contradicts a specific work.

## It's working if

- Every statement traces to the paper and the word limit is met.
- Abbreviations are defined at first use.

## Where it fits

Written after the paper, before the title. Map: [router](./science-research-writing.md).
