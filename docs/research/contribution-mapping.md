## What it does

Relates findings to the existing research map (confirms, extends, contradicts, differs in method), states the achievement, refines implications with reasons, and bounds novelty and applications.

It explains why a result occurred instead of repeating it.

## When to reach for it

- **Invocation.** Model-invoked: the agent reaches for it when the task fits, or you can name it.
- **Trigger boundary.** Reach for this in the middle and end of a Discussion, or on any sentence beginning "These findings".

## Common questions

**Which studies should I map onto?**
Those introduced earlier; many new studies in the Discussion is unusual.

## It's working if

- The reader can see where the work fits and what is new.
