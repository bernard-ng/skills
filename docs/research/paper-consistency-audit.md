## What it does

Audits a whole manuscript across sections: aims to methods to results to conclusions, plus numbers, terms, figures, and citations.

It reports conflicts by location and asks the author; it never silently harmonises disagreeing numbers.

## When to reach for it

- **Invocation.** Model-invoked: the agent reaches for it when the task fits, or you can name it.
- **Trigger boundary.** Reach for this before submission or after sections were revised at different times. For one section, use that section's skill.

## Common questions

**Is this the same as style editing?**
No. This finds substantive mismatches (120 samples versus 118 analysed). Style editing runs after them, on language.

## It's working if

- Every aim has a method, a result, and a conclusion in the trace table.
- Abstract, Title, and Discussion claims never exceed the Results.
