## What it does

Creates a precise, searchable, neutral title by turning the research question into a noun phrase and checking its noun stacks and prepositions.

It names what was actually studied and carries no caveats or value words.

## When to reach for it

- **Invocation.** Model-invoked: the agent reaches for it when the task fits, or you can name it.
- **Trigger boundary.** Reach for this when naming or retitling a paper. Noun-phrase ambiguity is handled by [noun-phrases-prepositions](./noun-phrases-prepositions.md).

## Common questions

**May a title state the result?**
Yes, if it is a real, directly supported achievement.

## It's working if

- Search terms appear in the title.
- Each noun and preposition has one reading.
