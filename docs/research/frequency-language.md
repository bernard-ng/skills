## What it does

Reports how often with counts and denominators first, and a deliberate word from the frequency scale when a word is needed.

It never substitutes a stronger term to persuade.

## When to reach for it

- **Invocation.** Model-invoked: the agent reaches for it when the task fits, or you can name it.
- **Trigger boundary.** Reach for this on "always", "often", "rarely", or a repeated step with no count.

## Common questions

**Is "often" objective?**
No, it is partly relative to what was expected.

## It's working if

- Wording matches numerator, denominator, and window.
