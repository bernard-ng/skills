## What it does

Decides a, an, the, or no article from what writer and reader both know, countability in context, and the claim the choice makes.

It does not use first-mention or fixed uncountable lists as rules.

## When to reach for it

- **Invocation.** Model-invoked: the agent reaches for it when the task fits, or you can name it.
- **Trigger boundary.** Reach for this on Methods text or text by writers whose first language lacks articles.

## Common questions

**Does "a" versus "the" really change meaning?**
Yes: "may hide a link" suggests one might exist; "the link" assumes it does.

## It's working if

- A reader can point to what "the" means.
