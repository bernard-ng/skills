## What it does

Shapes paragraphs around one job each, opening with a topic sentence, general before specific, and passing the first-sentence skim test.

It has no fixed length rule.

## When to reach for it

- **Invocation.** Model-invoked: the agent reaches for it when the task fits, or you can name it.
- **Trigger boundary.** Reach for this when a section is a list of sentences or paragraphs are fragmented or too long.

## Common questions

**How long should a paragraph be?**
As long as one idea needs. Split when the job changes.

## It's working if

- Reading only the first sentences gives the argument.
