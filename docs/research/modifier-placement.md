## What it does

Places adverbs and phrases next to the word they modify and splits sentences with competing modifiers.

It reads each sentence under the most plausible wrong attachment.

## When to reach for it

- **Invocation.** Model-invoked: the agent reaches for it when the task fits, or you can name it.
- **Trigger boundary.** Reach for this on dense sentences with "with", "only", "using", or several time and place phrases.

## Common questions

**Is a grammatical sentence safe?**
Not if the modifier attaches to the wrong word.

## It's working if

- No sentence needs a second reading to find the scope.
