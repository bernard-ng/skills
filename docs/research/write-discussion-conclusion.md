## What it does

Writes the Discussion or Conclusion as the Introduction in reverse: revisit, map onto existing research, state the contribution and its implications, then limitations and future work.

It makes the contribution and implications explicit instead of restating results.

## When to reach for it

- **Invocation.** Model-invoked: the agent reaches for it when the task fits, or you can name it.
- **Trigger boundary.** Reach for this when interpreting findings or closing the paper. For the study-to-literature comparison alone, use [contribution-mapping](./contribution-mapping.md).

## Common questions

**How do I choose what to revisit first?**
The most significant thing: the gap if answering it is the contribution, the method if the method is, or the results if they confirm or reveal something.

**Can I claim a first?**
Only after a thorough search, and still with "to our knowledge".

## It's working if

- The section answers the stated aim.
- Finding, interpretation, and speculation are distinguishable.

## Where it fits
