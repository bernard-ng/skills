## What it does

Chooses past simple, present simple, or present perfect by what the sentence claims: a completed event, an established fact, or a link to now.

Tense shifts are treated as meaningful and checked.

## When to reach for it

- **Invocation.** Model-invoked: the agent reaches for it when the task fits, or you can name it.
- **Trigger boundary.** Reach for this whenever tense affects the claim, or the user asks about tense.

## Common questions

**Can I report my own finding in present simple?**
Yes, to present it as established. Past simple keeps it local to the study.

## It's working if

- Every tense shift matches a change of function.
