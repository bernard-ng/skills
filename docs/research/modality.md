## What it does

Chooses modal verbs by function (ability, possibility, probability, near-certainty, advice, necessity) and handles past and negative forms.

It keeps may not, cannot, must not, and need not apart.

## When to reach for it

- **Invocation.** Model-invoked: the agent reaches for it when the task fits, or you can name it.
- **Trigger boundary.** Reach for this on any may, might, could, can, must, or should.

## Common questions

**Why is "must" risky?**
Used for inference it signals near-certainty without direct proof.

## It's working if

- No reader can mistake advice for inference or permission for possibility.
