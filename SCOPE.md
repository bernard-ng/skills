# Scope

This repository publishes agent procedures for English scientific research writing: rhetorical structure of each paper section, precise language, evidence and attribution checks, reproducibility, and manuscript review. The skills do not supply scientific findings, replace journal instructions, or replace field expertise.

In scope: skills that react to something visible in research text or a writing request, and that improve a draft's accuracy, clarity, or fit to its venue. Out of scope decisions are recorded in [.out-of-scope/](.out-of-scope/), each with the reason.

Judge new ideas against this file. Engineering skills are reserved for a future bucket and are not accepted into `research/`.
