# Scope

This repository publishes focused agent procedures for research writing and software engineering. Add a skill when a distinct, recurring decision benefits from guidance that an agent would not reliably infer from the task alone. Out-of-scope decisions are recorded in [.out-of-scope/](.out-of-scope/).

## Research writing

The research bucket covers rhetorical structure, precise language, evidence and attribution checks, reproducibility, and manuscript review. It does not supply scientific findings, replace journal instructions, or replace field expertise.

## Engineering

The engineering bucket applies responsibility-driven object design to framing a problem, finding and naming roles, assigning duties, tracing collaborations, choosing control style and layers, weighing patterns, reliability, justified flexibility, design review, and describing designs. It also covers domain-driven design: a shared domain language, entities, values and services, aggregates with factories and repositories, explicit rules and specifications, supple design, bounded contexts, core-domain focus, and large-scale structure. It is language-neutral and does not prescribe a programming language, a universal architecture, UML notation, or a design pattern.
