# Scientific conventions

Small conventions signal care, and inconsistency makes readers doubt the data.

## Units and symbols

- Use SI symbols, with a space between number and symbol (25 mL, 10 kg).
- SI symbols are symbols, not abbreviations: no full stop, and no plural s (5 kg, not 5 kgs).
- Check the symbol and its case against SI and the journal: s (not sec), h (not hr), kg (not Kg).
- Keep units consistent within a comparison and report precision consistently.
- Give a unit for every measured value and the unit basis for each percentage.

## Abbreviations and acronyms

- Define at first use ("hydroxyapatite (HA)"). Define again in the Abstract if used there, because the abstract stands alone.
- Use one abbreviation per concept, and do not introduce one that is used fewer than about three times.
- Choose a or an by sound: a UV lamp, an MRI scan, an LED array.
- Avoid abbreviations in titles unless they are universally understood.
- Use Latin abbreviations sparingly and correctly: e.g. (for example), i.e. (that is), et al. (and others), cf. (compare), vs. (against), ca. (about), N.B. (note well). Do not use e.g. and i.e. interchangeably.

## Singular and plural forms

| Singular | Plural |
| --- | --- |
| analysis, hypothesis, basis, axis, thesis | analyses, hypotheses, bases, axes, theses |
| criterion, phenomenon | criteria, phenomena |
| bacterium, medium, stimulus, spectrum | bacteria, media, spectra, stimuli |
| nucleus, locus, radius | nuclei, loci, radii |
| matrix, index, vortex, appendix | matrices, indices or indexes, vortices, appendices |
| formula | formulae or formulas |

"Data" is treated as plural in many journals and singular in others; follow the journal and stay consistent. Check agreement with the verb and demonstrative ("these data", "this criterion").

## Other consistency checks

Decimal and thousands separators, significant figures, hyphenation of prefixed terms (follow the journal and dictionary), capitalisation of terms, and percent versus percentage points ([quantity](../quantity-comparisons/SKILL.md)).

## Guardrails

Do not change values, units, or symbols silently. Flag conflicts with the journal guide rather than imposing a style.

## Check

Are all abbreviations defined, units formatted and consistent, plurals correct, and journal style followed?

**Illustrative example:** "Samples (n = 18) were centrifuged at 4000 g for 10 min" is cleaner than "18 samples were centrifuged for 10 mins".
