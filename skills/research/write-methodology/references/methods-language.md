# Methods language bank

Adapt to the study; never state a detail you were not given.

## Overview and sources

- Scope: "All tests were run in", "Most samples were", "In each case", "Both experiments used".
- Source: "was obtained from", "was purchased from", "was supplied by", "was used as supplied", "was synthesised according to", "is commercially available".
- Present simple for a standard method, instrument, or general background; past simple for what was done.

## Specific verbs (instead of "was done")

adjusted, assembled, calibrated, collected, computed, controlled, diluted, extracted, filtered, incorporated, isolated, maintained, measured, normalised, optimised, plotted, prepared, quantified, recorded, removed, repeated, sampled, selected, simulated, stabilised, transferred, treated, varied.

## Justifying a choice

- Purpose: "in order to", "so as to", "to", "with the aim of", "in an attempt to".
- Result of the choice: "thereby reducing", "which allowed", "which avoided", "making it possible to".
- Reason: "was chosen because", "was selected on the basis of", "for simplicity".
- Noun, infinitive, and -ing forms differ: "to reduce contamination", "reducing contamination", "the reduction of contamination". Check each fits the sentence.

## Signalling care (only if true)

carefully, thoroughly, firmly, tightly, directly, separately, independently, immediately, repeatedly, at least three times, constantly, randomly, vigorously. Attach a number or condition where one exists.

## Relating to other studies

| Relation | Wording |
| --- | --- |
| Identical | "as described by X", "following the method of X", "according to X", "as previously reported" |
| Similar | "adapted from X", "with minor modifications", "essentially as in X, except", "a modified version of" |
| Different | "modified as follows", "differs from X in that", "a new step was added" |

State the differences when they are significant, and say why they improve or suit the study.

## Problems

- Say what happened: "did not align precisely", "was limited by", "could not be measured below".
- Bound it: "only approximate", "affected two of 18 samples", "variation was negligible" (only with evidence).
- Offer a way forward: "this was corrected by", "future work should" (invitation) versus "future work will" (own plan).
