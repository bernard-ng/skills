# Research writing skills

35 skills for writing and reviewing English scientific papers and theses. Every skill here is **model-invoked**: its description tells the agent which text signals and requests should fire it, so the agent reaches for it without being asked. You can also name a skill directly.

Start with [science-research-writing](science-research-writing/SKILL.md), the router. It sets the drafting order, owns the table of signals that fire each specialist, and is the map of the set. Each skill has a human-facing page under [docs/research](../../docs/research/science-research-writing.md).

These skills are writing procedures, not a source of facts. Supply the actual study details, data, references, and journal requirements. All examples are illustrative.

**Model-invoked**

### Orchestration

- **[science-research-writing](science-research-writing/SKILL.md)**: The router. Plan, draft, and revise a research paper, and send each decision to the right specialist.
- **[paper-consistency-audit](paper-consistency-audit/SKILL.md)**: Audit a whole manuscript for alignment of aims, methods, results, conclusions, figures, terms, and citations.
- **[target-article-analysis](target-article-analysis/SKILL.md)**: Learn a journal or field's conventions from sample articles and the author guide, and build a move model and phrase bank.

### Sections

- **[write-introduction](write-introduction/SKILL.md)**: Draft or revise an Introduction: importance, prior work, a supported gap, and the aim.
- **[write-methodology](write-methodology/SKILL.md)**: Draft or revise Methods that let a reader repeat the work and accept it.
- **[write-results](write-results/SKILL.md)**: Draft or revise Results with interpretation, comparisons, uncertainty, and display references.
- **[write-discussion-conclusion](write-discussion-conclusion/SKILL.md)**: Interpret results, map them onto prior work, bound the contribution, and close with limits and next steps.
- **[write-abstract](write-abstract/SKILL.md)**: Draft or shorten a stand-alone abstract from the finished paper.
- **[write-title](write-title/SKILL.md)**: Create or revise a precise, searchable, neutral title.

### Research and evidence

- **[literature-review-positioning](literature-review-positioning/SKILL.md)**: Organise prior work around the paper's question as a short research map.
- **[formulate-research-gap](formulate-research-gap/SKILL.md)**: Formulate a defensible gap, problem, or prediction and connect it to the aim.
- **[methods-reproducibility](methods-reproducibility/SKILL.md)**: Audit Methods for missing, vague, or inconsistent detail.
- **[figure-table-commentary](figure-table-commentary/SKILL.md)**: Write and check callouts, captions, and the prose around figures and tables.
- **[citation-attribution](citation-attribution/SKILL.md)**: Tie claims about prior work, methods, and novelty to verified sources.
- **[limitations-future-work](limitations-future-work/SKILL.md)**: Report problems where they arise, bound them honestly, and turn them into next steps.
- **[contribution-mapping](contribution-mapping/SKILL.md)**: Relate findings to existing research and state the achievement, implications, and applications.
- **[evaluative-language](evaluative-language/SKILL.md)**: Say what numbers mean, anchored to a benchmark, without bare numbers or inflation.

### Language and style

- **[grammar-and-meaning](grammar-and-meaning/SKILL.md)**: Edit for grammar and for invisible errors that change the scientific meaning.
- **[tense-selection](tense-selection/SKILL.md)**: Choose past, present, or present perfect by what each claim asserts.
- **[signalling-transitions](signalling-transitions/SKILL.md)**: Connect sentences and ideas with connectors that name the true relation.
- **[active-passive-voice](active-passive-voice/SKILL.md)**: Choose voice so the actor, and whose work it is, stays clear.
- **[paragraph-coherence](paragraph-coherence/SKILL.md)**: Shape paragraphs around one job each, with topic sentences and logical order.
- **[articles-determiners](articles-determiners/SKILL.md)**: Correct a, an, the, and zero article from reference and countability.
- **[modifier-placement](modifier-placement/SKILL.md)**: Place adverbs and phrases so their scope is unambiguous.
- **[noun-phrases-prepositions](noun-phrases-prepositions/SKILL.md)**: Untangle noun stacks and ambiguous prepositions in titles, captions, and dense sentences.
- **[sequence-language](sequence-language/SKILL.md)**: Describe order, timing, and overlap of steps precisely.
- **[frequency-language](frequency-language/SKILL.md)**: Report how often with counts, denominators, and the right frequency word.
- **[quantity-comparisons](quantity-comparisons/SKILL.md)**: Report quantities and comparisons with exact units, comparators, and direction.
- **[causal-language](causal-language/SKILL.md)**: Match causal wording to the design and evidence.
- **[modality](modality/SKILL.md)**: Choose modal verbs for possibility, ability, probability, advice, and necessity.
- **[claim-strength](claim-strength/SKILL.md)**: Calibrate hedging and certainty to the evidence and keep it consistent.
- **[academic-vocabulary](academic-vocabulary/SKILL.md)**: Select precise, conventional academic words and verbs by function.
- **[scientific-conventions](scientific-conventions/SKILL.md)**: Check units, symbols, abbreviations, Latin terms, and Latin and Greek plurals.
- **[spatial-description](spatial-description/SKILL.md)**: Describe the layout, position, and connections of apparatus precisely.
- **[scientific-style-editing](scientific-style-editing/SKILL.md)**: Run the final four-pass edit for structure, meaning, language, and conventions.
